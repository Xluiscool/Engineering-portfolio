### Research Findings

#### Finding 1: [Plastic waste and U.S. recycling statistics]

**What I found:**  

I found that the U.S. made about 35.7 million tons of plastic waste in 2018, but only about 8.7% of it was recycled. A lot more plastic ended up in landfills.

**Why it matters to our project:**  

This matters because our recycling center should not try to recycle every type of plastic. We should find plastic that our school has a lot of and that we can sort and recycle correctly.

**Source:**  
[Plastic waste and U.S. recycling statistics]([https://www.epa.gov/facts-and-figures-about-materials-waste-and-recycling/national-overview-facts-and-figures-materials])

#### Finding 3: [Why aren’t we recycling more plastic?]

**What I found:**  

Plastic recycling proved to be harder than it first might appear—different plastics have to be sorted, and plastic can be contaminated by food, labels, or other materials.

**Why it matter to our project:**  

This is important because we need a good system for collecting, cleaning and sorting plastics before recycling. We should teach people what kinds of plastic our recycling center accepts; otherwise they place the wrong things in the bins.

**Source:**  
[Why aren’t we recycling more plastic?] ([https://stories.undp.org/why-arent-we-recycling-more-plastic])
